Important transcription decisions
- Motor current, speed, efficiency, mass and locked-rotor current remain tied
  to the specified four-pole example; the six TCA schedule rows stay separate.
- Power, airflow, pressure and torque maxima are not presented as simultaneous.
- Gearbox torque stays in kNm, not Nm. Worked conveyor calculations are excluded.
- Series 70 flow keeps its 6 bar supply / 1 bar pressure-drop condition and port
  mapping. EB 80 0.3 W is holding power after the switching pulse.
- H20 accuracy includes the fixed velocity term, as in the guide and the linked
  manufacturer sheet. The previous incomplete 0.5% statement is replaced.
- LOGOSCREEN physical terminals and internally recorded/interface-fed signals
  use separate rows. Code 888 is described as optional functionality, not as
  an unconditional compliance certification.
- Thermocouple 901120 now includes the supplied lower bound. 901250 separates
  Type K from J/L limits and keeps the sheath/diameter qualification.
- The guide identifies ALTRA as Premium Altra ALM; the public listing name and
  existing brand label are preserved. Exact unit size still needs a nameplate.
- Market statistics, hypothetical payback, worked force/power examples, supplier
  contacts and instructions addressed to a buyer are not product specifications
  and were not imported into the catalog.
