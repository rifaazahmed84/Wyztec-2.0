WYZTEC PRODUCT CONTENT REVIEW
Reviewed: 2026-11-23

Scope
All 31 active products now have an individual tagline, overview, four key features,
four applications, relevant industry tags and a practical selection guide.
Content is maintained in server/src/data/product-content.ts and applied to the
final deduplicated catalog in products.ts. Adding a new product also requires an
editorial entry keyed by its active slug; the API tests check complete coverage.

Evidence and interpretation
Manufacturer pages below support product-family or named-model descriptions.
Application examples and the site's eight industry categories are editorial
mappings of those capabilities, not manufacturer certifications or a statement
that every variant is suitable for every environment. Exact duty and configuration
still need selection. No new numerical performance ratings, warranty promises,
stock claims, certificates or guaranteed savings have been added.

Identity and images were kept. The subsequent buyer-guide import replaces the
old specification defaults and expands the thermocouple model table; see
PRODUCT-SPECIFICATIONS-REVIEW.txt for current data and scope. Neither editorial
copy nor a family range certifies an exact order code. Brand and series details
cannot be established from photos alone.
